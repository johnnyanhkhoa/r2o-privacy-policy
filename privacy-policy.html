Privacy Policy — R2O Client App

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rent 2 Own ("R2O", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the R2O Client App (the "App"), a hire-purchase (rent-to-own) financing service available in Myanmar. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide directly

  • Identity information: full name, phone number, National Registration Card (NRC) number, and a photo/scan of your NRC card.
  • Biometric-adjacent verification photo: a selfie photo taken during signup, used to verify your identity against your NRC record.
  • Account security: a 6-digit PIN you create (stored in encrypted/hashed form — we never store your PIN in plain text).
  • Contract & financial information: details of your hire-purchase contract (asset financed, installment schedule, amounts due, payment history), and any support messages or feedback you send us (e.g. via chat or service ratings).

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device information: device model, manufacturer, operating system and version, app version.
  • Push notification token: a device token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) used to deliver payment reminders and service notifications.
  • Usage and log data: app interactions, crash logs, and IP address (collected automatically by our servers when the App communicates with our backend), used for security, fraud prevention, and troubleshooting.

1.3 Information from payment transactions

When you make a payment through the App, you are directed to a supported payment gateway (AYA Pay, CTZPay, OK$, or Wave Money). We receive transaction confirmation details (amount, status, reference number, timestamp) from these gateways to update your payment record. We do not collect or store your payment gateway account credentials, PINs, or full card/account numbers — these are handled directly and securely by the respective payment provider.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, and verify your identity during signup.
  • To administer your hire-purchase contract, process payments, and maintain accurate payment records.
  • To send you service-related notifications (payment reminders, receipts, contract updates, promotional offers where applicable).
  • To provide customer support, including through our in-app AI assistant and, when needed, human support agents.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and other illegal activity.
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
  • To evaluate your eligibility for optional programs, such as our Loyalty rewards program, based on your payment record.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not share your data with advertisers for third-party advertising purposes.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal and contract information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward, as needed to comply with our legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted network communication (HTTPS), secure on-device storage for sensitive data (via encrypted local storage), and access controls on our backend systems. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • You may review and update certain personal information from the Profile section of the App.
  • You may enable or disable biometric login and push notifications from the App's Settings.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below, subject to our legal and contractual obligations (for example, we may need to retain contract and payment records while a contract is active).

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by individuals who are at least 18 years old and legally able to enter into a hire-purchase agreement. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the App or by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
