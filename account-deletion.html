Account & Data Deletion — R2O Client App

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Rent 2 Own (R2O) is committed to giving you control over your personal data. This page explains how to request deletion of your R2O Client App account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

The R2O Client App does not currently offer in-app self-service account deletion. To request deletion of your account, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact us at support@r2o.com.mm, or use the in-app Support Chat (Profile → App Tutorial is not required — simply open the chat icon from the Home screen).
  2. Send a request to delete your account, including the phone number registered on your R2O account, so we can verify your identity.
  3. We will confirm your identity and process your request. You will receive a confirmation once your account has been deleted.
Processing time: account deletion requests are typically completed within 30 days of identity verification.

What Data Is Deleted

Upon a verified deletion request, we permanently delete or anonymize the following data associated with your account:

  • Your name, phone number, and NRC number
  • Your NRC photo and selfie verification photo
  • Your login PIN
  • Your device push-notification token
  • Your in-app support chat history

What Data Is Retained, and Why

Some data cannot be deleted immediately upon request, as we are required to retain it to comply with applicable financial recordkeeping, accounting, and legal obligations related to hire-purchase (rent-to-own) contracts:

DataRetentionReason
Contract records (asset financed, installment schedule) Retained for the period required by applicable financial and tax regulations Legal and accounting compliance for an active or completed lending agreement
Payment transaction history Retained for the period required by applicable financial and tax regulations Financial recordkeeping, dispute resolution, and fraud prevention

If you have an active (unpaid or ongoing) contract at the time of your request, we may not be able to delete your account until the contract is settled or terminated, since your account is needed to manage that ongoing financial obligation. We will inform you if this applies to your request.
